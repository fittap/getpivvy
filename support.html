Support

How can we help?

Email us at support@getpivvy.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Report a post

Tap the share arrow under the post, then tap Report post.

Report a comment

Tap the ••• next to the comment, or press and hold it, then tap Report comment.

Report or block an account

Open their profile, tap the ••• at the top, then tap Report profile or Block account. They're never told. To unblock someone, go to Settings, then Blocked accounts.

Comments on your posts

You can delete any comment on your own posts from its ••• menu, and turn comments off on any of your posts with the Comments on switch.

Delete your account

Go to Settings, then Delete account. Everything is hidden right away and deleted for good after 30 days. If you change your mind, log back in before then to restore it.

Something not working?

Email support@getpivvy.com with what happened, what you were doing, and your iPhone model. A screenshot helps a lot.

Someone in danger?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number first.
