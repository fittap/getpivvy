Community Rules

A kind place to talk about clothes

Pivvy is about how clothes fit, never about whose body is better. Everyone agrees to these rules when they sign up.

No comments about bodies or weight

Don't comment on anyone's body, shape, or weight. Talk about the clothes: the fit, the fabric, the sizing. Clothing words like "skinny jeans" or "too big" are fine.

Sizes are just numbers

Heights, sizes, and measurements are here to help people find clothes that fit. No size is better or worse than another. Pivvy never ranks or sorts anyone by body type or weight.

Be kind and respectful

No insults, harassment, or hateful language toward anyone.

Be honest about your sizing

Shoppers count on your height, sizes, and measurements to decide what fits them. Share your real sizing and the real size you wore.

Adults only

You must be 18 or older to use Pivvy.

Breaking the rules gets you removed

Comments that break these rules are taken down, and accounts that break them are removed from Pivvy.

Tools to keep you safe

  • A filter hides common body insults before they're ever posted.
  • Report any post, comment, or profile. Reports go privately to the Pivvy team.
  • Block anyone. They're never told, and you won't see each other's posts or profile.
  • Delete comments on your posts, or turn comments off on any post.

See Support for how to use them.
