Our Story

Why we made Pivvy

I’m Zeha, a college student in North Dakota. Like a lot of people, I’d see a creator in an outfit I loved, order my usual size, and send it back because it didn’t fit. Creators show the clothes but almost never share their height, size, or measurements, so shoppers are left guessing.

So I started building Pivvy. On Pivvy, every creator shares their real sizing, and shoppers can find people built like them. No guessing, no judging, just real bodies and real fits.

Pivvy is in beta now, and we’re looking for our first creators and testers to help shape it.
